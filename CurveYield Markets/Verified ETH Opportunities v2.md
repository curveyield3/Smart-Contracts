A finite search cannot prove global nonexistence; "likely unlisted" is stated only when supported.

**Source versus dapp:** The source TVL and APY remain frozen from the CurveYield discovery snapshot. Dapp figures are later live observations (November 17, ~16:35–16:40 UTC) and can differ. Never confuse an underlying strategy vault's TVL, collateral-only balances, global protocol totals, market liquidity, current spot APY, 30-day APY, base-asset interest and native-ETH deposit capacity. All discrepancies are labeled. Entries may be duplicate index records representing one actual dapp vault; do not sum their TVLs. The index-only UUID alone does not verify direct deposit compatibility.
